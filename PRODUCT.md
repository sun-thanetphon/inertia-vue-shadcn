# Product Context: Clinic Operations Platform

## Register
product

## Description
A modern dental clinic management platform built with Laravel 12, Inertia.js, Vue 3, Tailwind CSS, and Shadcn Vue primitives. The platform enables multi-branch scheduling, patient treatment tracking, doctor queues, and billing receipt generation.

## Audience & Users
- Primary: Clinic front-desk coordinators, receptionists, clinic managers, and dentists.
- Usage context: Fast-paced clinic front counter and doctor consultation rooms. Focused, high-frequency, requiring immediate clarity, zero visual clutter, and instant feedback.

## Design Direction & System
- Register: `product` (Function-first, high density with generous breathing room, crisp type, semantic states).
- Color Strategy: Restrained (Zinc/Slate structural neutrals with dedicated medical emerald `#059669` as primary brand accent, amber `#d97706` for pending actions, and branch identity dots).
- Typography: Inter / system-ui, fixed rem hierarchy, tight letter-spacing (-0.02em to -0.03em for headings).
